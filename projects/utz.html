What I Built

My team prepared a sell-side recommendation for Utz Brands, including company and industry overview, deal structure, buyer rationale, valuation summary, downside risks, and merger considerations.

Methods

  • Discounted cash flow analysis
  • Comparable companies analysis
  • Precedent transactions analysis
  • Football field valuation summary
  • Risk and mitigation analysis

Deal Framing

The recommendation analyzed why Intersnack would value Utz as a U.S. market entry platform, including direct-store-delivery routes, domestic manufacturing, brand positioning, and access to the U.S. salty snack category.
