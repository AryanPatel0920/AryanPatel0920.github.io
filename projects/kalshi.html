Kalshi NBA Finals Activation

A live activation concept designed to drive Kalshi app downloads, first trades, and social sharing by combining NBA Finals watch-party traffic with a branded basketball shooting challenge and prediction market education.

Concept

The idea places portable basketball shooting booths near high-traffic NBA Finals watch zones in New York City. Fans sign up through a QR code, compete in a shooting challenge, and learn how prediction markets can connect to live sports outcomes.

Activation Mechanics

  • Portable basketball booth with Kalshi branding
  • QR-code onboarding before participating
  • Ambassadors explaining the game, app, and first-trade flow
  • Merch and social-post incentives tied to participation

Budget and KPIs

The concept estimated a $6K-8K activation budget and tracked success through daily foot traffic, QR scans, app downloads, first trades, social tags, cost per acquisition, and earned impressions.
