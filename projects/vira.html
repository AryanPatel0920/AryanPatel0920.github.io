VIRA AI Fashion Agent

VIRA is an AI fashion agent concept built for busy parents who want simpler outfit coordination, wardrobe tracking, shopping support, and clear style recommendations without spending hours comparing options.

Open full PDF Back to projects

Pitch deck

Read the VIRA deck directly here.

Problem

The pitch focused on busy parents who face burnout, limited personal-care time, and friction around shopping and outfit decisions. VIRA framed fashion as a time and confidence problem, not just a clothing problem.

Product

  • Outfit coordination based on calendar and household needs
  • Wardrobe tracking and care guidance
  • Shopping agent with filtering and discount matching
  • Personalized recommendations with simple decision support

Business Case

The deck outlined free, premium, and family subscription tiers, affiliate revenue, sponsored listings, market sizing, user acquisition assumptions, lifetime value, and a funding ask tied to MVP, beta launch, security, marketing, and public launch goals.

Traction and Role

The project included waitlist validation, social media traction, 100+ sign-ups, and a planned beta cohort. As CFO, I helped shape the financial story, unit economics, funding needs, and investor-facing pitch logic.
