What I Built

My team recommended a long position in Micron at $465.66 with a $675 price target by EOY CY 2027, implying 45% upside. The pitch framed Micron as a beneficiary of AI-led memory demand and a domestic U.S. semiconductor supply asset.

Methods

  • Financial forecast and valuation football field
  • DRAM, NAND, HBM, and SOCAMM2 market research
  • Supply-demand and inventory cycle analysis
  • Risk, catalyst, and contract visibility analysis

Core Thesis

The deck argues that Micron benefits from an AI-driven memory wall, contracted HBM revenue, Nvidia platform exposure, and a strategic U.S. manufacturing position supported by CHIPS Act incentives and hyperscaler demand for supply security.

Catalysts

Key catalysts included FQ3-26 earnings, easing CHIPS Act buyback restrictions, HBM4E ramp visibility, and additional multi-year supply commitment agreements that could reduce the stock's cyclicality discount.
